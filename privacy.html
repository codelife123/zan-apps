Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-04-21

This application respects your privacy. To provide social features like our global leaderboard, we collect and store certain information as outlined below.

Information Collection and Use

We collect your user name, device ID, and game scores. This information is used exclusively to display your ranking and performance on our global leaderboard. We do not collect other personal data such as email addresses, phone numbers, or location.

Data Retention

The data we collect (user name, device ID, and game scores) is kept indefinitely to maintain the integrity of our historical global leaderboards. We do not delete this data.

Automatically Collected Information

The app accesses your device ID for authentication and to associate your scores with your profile on the leaderboard.

Data Sharing

Your user name and game scores are publicly visible on our global leaderboard. We do not sell or share your data with external third parties for marketing purposes.

Security

We transmit your user name, device ID, and scores to our secure backend servers to power the leaderboard. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
